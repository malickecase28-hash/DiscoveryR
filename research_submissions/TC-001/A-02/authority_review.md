# Authority review

The V2 evidence directly establishes only declared serialization structure for the six mandatory topics: variant names and field paths. Each topic is represented in `authority_candidate.json` by an `AUTHORITATIVE` structure claim.

The evidence does not establish event or state meaning, scientific role, direction, baseline, threshold semantics, reset, persistence, history dependence, availability, causal timing, or payload keying. Field names and physical row co-location were not treated as proof. Physical output, occurrence clock, and availability clock remain distinct and unresolved.

No empirical, runtime, raw-data, confirmation, or implementation evidence was consulted. No activation or behavioral conclusion is made.
