# Agent operations

Research work is split by responsibility, not by duplicated scientific worlds.
The default team is one deterministic toolsmith/scanner, two independent blind
interpreters, and one method challenger. Agent count is an orchestration
parameter and never evidence of validity.

Each assignment names its scope, allowed artifacts, instrument and exposure
class. Workers receive no prior outcome winners and cannot read peer, private,
future, or holdout surfaces outside their declared permission. Provider
pseudonymity and peer isolation are preserved where the workflow requires them.

Submissions are immutable, content-hashed, and published only after validation.
The method challenger is independent of discovery and confirmation. A worker
cannot self-confirm, alter a frozen contract, unlock confirmation, or rewrite a
knowledge record. Partial, null, rejected, contradictory, and inconclusive
submissions remain queryable.

The orchestration layer should stop when its assigned artifact and checks are
complete. It must not add agents, gates, adapters, or services without a named
failure they prevent and a synthetic acceptance test.
